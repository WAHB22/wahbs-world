System overview

One module per machine type. Each box protects the machine's health and the resources it consumes, gas, wire, filament and laser tube time, and reports to one owner app. Every machine gets a QR manual.

Concept only: not built or tested. Every number and alert is illustrative.

One session, five steps

Same on every box

  1. 1

    Tap fob

    The box checks that machine's trained list. No fob on the list, no power.

  2. 2

    On

    Power, gas or the laser's enable line switches on for this member.

  3. 3

    Watch

    Current, sensors and resources are read every second against the learned normal.

  4. 4

    Alert or act

    Gas leak, fire or no exhaust: the machine stops first, then the owner is told. Anything else: Shut down, Call technician or Mark as OK.

  5. 5

    Off and log

    Tap out or idle timeout. Who, how long and what was used go to the member's record.

Same rules on every box

  1. NFC fob, not a PINA round contactless reader. "Tap your fob", then "Checking fob", then "Hi, name".
  2. Trained listA fob that is not on the machine's list sees "Not trained on this machine" and the machine stays locked.
  3. QR manualA printed sticker beside the reader opens that machine's manual.
  4. Learning modeThe first two weeks only record the normal range, with no automatic shutdown.
  5. One alert formatShut down, Call technician or Mark as OK. Safety events stop the machine first.
  6. Idle timeoutNothing happening for the time the owner sets: everything switches off.
  7. Resources per sessionWho, how long and the resource used, added to the member's record.
  8. Illustrative labelsEvery chart, number and alert in these drawings is illustrative, not measured.
