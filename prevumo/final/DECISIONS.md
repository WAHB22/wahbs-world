# PREVUMO final: the three decisions (loop 15)

Wahb asked for three calls made without asking him. Each is written down here with the reason and where it shows.

## 1. Keep the free pilot interest, and say exactly how strong it is

**Call:** slide 6 keeps "Makerspaces and Fab Labs we spoke with were open to a free pilot", and slide 7 keeps "Customers: interest in a free pilot, nothing signed". Nobody says "many", "signed" or "partners".

**Why:** it comes from the team's own interviews and it is the only sign of pull the project has, so cutting it would undersell the work. But no names are on file, so the deck claims interest and nothing more, and the team adds names only if it has them (Q3, Q30, the script's "before Friday" box).

## 2. Tell the truth on stage, even where it costs

**Call:** the talk says out loud that nothing is built or measured yet (slide 6); the roadmap puts approval before the pilot, makes the pilot watch only, and leaves the welder for last, although the welder is the star of the films (slide 9); and PREVUMO sits next to Fabman instead of claiming no competition (slide 7).

**Why:** a final presentation is judged by people who will ask exactly these questions. In Ontario a product must be approved before it is used (ESA), so a box that switches a 240 V welder in a free pilot would be the first thing a judge attacks. Saying it first turns each weak point into a plan, and every claim left on a slide has a source in FACTS.md.

## 3. Name organizations, not people, anywhere public

**Call:** the public site no longer names the Yukonstruct contact; the quote is credited to "Yukonstruct (Oct 27)", as the deck's "owner quotes anonymized" promises. The organization stays named on slides 15 and 17.

**Why:** the QR on the last slide sends judges straight to the site, and nobody on file confirmed that this person agreed to be quoted by name. Removing a name is easy to undo if he agrees; publishing one without consent is not. The PD B3 report, already submitted, is left as it was.
