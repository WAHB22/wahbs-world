      How it works

        About this prototype

        A concept for PREVUMO's module on an enclosed, Ultimaker-style 3D printer (PD B2: one module per machine): a box frame whose glass plate drops as the print grows. It has not been built or tested. The box follows the deck: keypad, current sensor and an ESP32 with Wi-Fi, with a certified relay inside rated up to 15 A at 120 V that switches the printer directly. So the box plugs into a normal wall outlet and the printer plugs into the box: no contactor and no gas. A printer draws the most current while its bed and nozzle heat up after it turns on. That warm-up is part of its learned normal range, so it never raises an alert. The chart and the alert are illustrations, not measured data, and the animation runs as a time-lapse.
