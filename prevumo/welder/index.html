        About this prototype

        A concept for PREVUMO's welder module (PD B2: one module per machine, gas and electric). It has not been built or tested. The box itself follows the deck: keypad, current sensor and an ESP32 with Wi-Fi. For a 240 V welder the box drives a contactor installed by a licensed electrician, and the box needs CSA certification before sale. The gas valve is the new part for welders. The chart and the alert are illustrations, not measured data.
