The token plan for WAHB'S WORLD, Phase 0

Night service at the pass. He opens the site before a 06:15 shift and again after a lecture that ends at 21:50. Both scenes are dark rooms, so the ground is deep navy, the light comes from the objects, and warm orange is the heat lamp that marks what needs him now.

Few colors, deeply satisfying

Navy carries most of every screen. Blue and cyan do the structural work. Orange appears once or twice per screen, on the one action or the one thing that is hot. Basil green and alarm coral only mark status.

Glass that stays readable

Text never sits on raw transparency. Each pane has an opaque enough tint, and its contrast is measured in the worst case: the pane over pure white light, the brightest thing a scene can put behind it.

Numbers are worst case contrast ratios from tools/contrast.py. AA needs 4.5 for body text. Signal and ember are too weak for small text on dark glass, so they are reserved for fills, lines and display sizes, where 3.0 applies.

Nine worlds, one family

Worlds are told apart by their scene and their objects. Each one shifts the accent within the family, nothing more. The list below is set the way the landing will list them: a menu, not a grid of cards.

Today

ember

The pass. Heat lamps over the one thing that matters now, tickets on the rail, a thumb dock of check ins.

School

coolant

A small plant. Fluid moving through a heat exchanger, a reactor whose conversion is progress, gauges, a pen writing.

Work

flame

The kitchen. Shifts as order tickets, steam, plates, a service bell, the 24 hour line.

Money

lagoon

The jar and the bill. A glass jar filling toward each goal, receipts, categories flowing as streams.

Projects

cobalt

Blueprints. Nodes and dependency lines; a finished milestone lights its sheet.

Career

sky

The chain. Experience to evidence to skill to story to opportunity, with the summer 2027 goal in view.

Knowledge

frost

A constellation. Topics as stars that connect; reviews that come due.

Training

signal

Kinetic. Movement trails, rings and progress arcs. Showing up is visible, without guilt.

Life

glow

Moments. A timeline where places, meals, people, good and hard days accumulate.

Intensity sets the tempo

Five levels change how much moves and how fast. Text size, contrast and layout never change with intensity. The cap in Settings always wins, and reduced motion replaces every move with a short crossfade.
